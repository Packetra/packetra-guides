# Packetra Privacy Policy (Service Overview)

## Privacy-first by design

Packetra is built for customers who value privacy and control.

- **Anonymous sign-up:** registration requires **email only**
- **Crypto payments:** we support **Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), USDT (TRC20), USDT (ERC20), Monero (XMR), and Litecoin (LTC)**
- **No unnecessary data collection:** we aim to collect only what is required to provide and support the service

## Payments

We offer both crypto and standard payment methods, including:

- Cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT TRC20, USDT ERC20, XMR, LTC)
- PayPal
- Wise  
(Manual card payments via PayPal can be arranged if needed.)

## Infrastructure & jurisdiction

- Servers are located in **Finland**
- Operated under EU data protection standards

## Support & access

- Support is provided via **24/7 ticket system**
- Customers manage services through the client portal and hosting control panel
- Optional **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** is available for account protection

## Notes

This page is a high-level overview of our privacy approach. For legal/privacy compliance text, we provide a separate formal privacy policy on our website.
