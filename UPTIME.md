# Packetra Uptime Policy

## Uptime Commitment

Packetra provides a 99.9% uptime guarantee across its hosting infrastructure.

Our systems are continuously monitored to ensure availability, performance, and stability.

## Monitoring

- 24/7 infrastructure monitoring
- Automated alerts for service disruptions
- Proactive response to hardware or network incidents

## Infrastructure

All servers are located in Finland in renewable energy powered datacenters.

Network-level DDoS protection is enabled across infrastructure to minimize service interruptions.

Dedicated servers are deployed with Software RAID-1 by default for redundancy.

## Incident Communication

In the event of an outage:

- Our team investigates immediately
- Updates are communicated via support tickets and status notifications
- We aim to restore services as quickly as possible

## Transparency

We believe in transparent operations. Any significant service incident is handled with clear communication and prompt technical resolution.
