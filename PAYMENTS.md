# Packetra Payment Options

## Cryptocurrency Payments

Packetra supports multiple cryptocurrency payment methods to provide flexibility and privacy for customers.

Supported crypto options:

- Bitcoin (BTC)
- Ethereum (ETH)
- USDT (TRC20)
- USDT (ERC20)
- Monero (XMR)
- Litecoin (LTC)

Crypto payments are processed securely and can be used for all hosting services.

## Traditional Payment Methods

We also support standard payment options:

- PayPal
- Wise
- Manual card payments (processed via PayPal upon request)

## Billing Cycles

Customers can choose from:

- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Semi-Annual
- Annual
- Biennial
- Triennial

Longer billing cycles may qualify for promotional benefits such as setup fee waivers on dedicated servers.

## Transparency

All pricing is displayed clearly in EUR before checkout. There are no hidden renewal fees.
